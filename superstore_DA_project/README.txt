# Superstore Sales & Profit Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Sample Superstore** dataset using **Power BI** to answer key business questions related to sales, profitability, customer behavior, and product performance.

The dashboard provides interactive visualizations that help management identify profitable regions, sales trends, customer segments, and top/bottom-performing products.

---

## Business Questions

The dashboard answers the following questions:

- Which regions and categories are the most profitable?
- What are the sales trends over time?
- Which customer segments perform the best?
- How can we identify top and bottom performers?

---

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Top Performing Region
- Top Performing Category

### Interactive Filters
- Year
- State
- Category

### Visualizations
- Sales Trend Over Time
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Top 10 Sub-Categories by Sales
- Bottom 10 Sub-Categories by Profit

### Executive Summary
A concise summary highlighting the major business insights derived from the dashboard.

---

## Key Insights

- West is the most profitable region.
- Technology is the highest-performing product category.
- Consumer is the strongest customer segment by sales.
- Sales show an overall upward trend over the years.
- Phones and Chairs generate the highest sales.
- Tables contribute the largest losses despite generating substantial sales.
